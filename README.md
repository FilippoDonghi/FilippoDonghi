# Hi, I'm Filippo

I'm a Software Engineering and Green IT student at the University of Amsterdam,
based in Amsterdam. I enjoy taking a project beyond the first working prototype:
making its assumptions visible, its setup reproducible, and its results easy to
evaluate.

## Selected work

### [Wine&Dine](https://github.com/FilippoDonghi/WineDine)

An Android team project for exploring wines, saving favourites, and discovering
food pairings and recipes. My original contributions covered dish and recipe
models, pairing experiments, dynamic rating and bottle-detail UI, and the profile
menu. The repository now also provides fixture-backed demo data, automated tests,
Android lint, and a reproducible CI build.

`Java` · `Android` · `Firebase` · `Room` · `Retrofit`

### [Hotel Search Learning to Rank](https://github.com/FilippoDonghi/data-mining-techniques)

An academic group case study comparing a popularity baseline with LightGBM
LambdaMART on hotel-search impressions. My documented role focused on exploratory
analysis support, reviewing plots and metrics, and final report editing and
presentation. The public repository includes a deterministic synthetic demo and
clearly separates historical results from reproducible evidence.

`Python` · `pandas` · `NumPy` · `LightGBM` · `Learning to rank`

## How I work

- I prefer small, verifiable changes backed by tests and automated checks.
- I document data, security, and reproducibility limits instead of hiding them.
- I care about efficient software and the environmental impact of computing.
- I credit team contributions precisely and distinguish evidence from claims.

I'm interested in software engineering work where reliability, thoughtful system
design, and measurable impact matter.
